WHY MASTI

Three reasons it disappears from the fridge fast

It's not just a drink — it's the thing that shows up at every lunch table from Ahmedabad to Chennai, for reasons that go beyond nostalgia.

Digestion

Settles a heavy meal

Traditional chaas has long been the go-to after a rich thali — the probiotics and light spicing help digestion do its job.

Hydration

Cools faster than water alone

The natural electrolytes in buttermilk replace what a hot afternoon takes out of you — colder, tangier, and more satisfying than plain water.

Everyday price

A treat, not a splurge

Sold in tetra packs and pouches from ₹10, Masti is priced to be a daily habit, not an occasional indulgence.
